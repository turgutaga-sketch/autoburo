# AI Business Operating System

## Product thesis

AI Business Operating System is a mobile-first, multi-company control plane for operating real businesses with AI agents, automations, structured data, approvals and measurable outcomes.

It is not a chatbot collection. It is an execution system.

## Core promise

> Think. Command. Approve. The system executes, measures and learns.

## Operating model

The platform separates each company legally, financially and operationally while presenting one owner-level command center.

### Company spaces

1. NWASB Werkstatt OS
2. Smart Parts 24
3. Global AI Media System
4. Music and Video Factory
5. Agency and Client OS
6. Future companies and brands

Each company has its own customers, permissions, files, workflows, accounting context and audit trail.

## Four-dimensional architecture

### 1. Operations

Customers, vehicles, jobs, appointments, parts, invoices, cash, content, channels, campaigns, projects and support.

### 2. Intelligence

An orchestrator decomposes goals into tasks and routes them to specialist agents. Agents are created on demand instead of running thousands of permanent processes.

### 3. Memory and data

Company rules, prices, templates, historical decisions, customer history, brand language and approval policies are stored as structured institutional memory.

### 4. Mobile command experience

The primary interface is an iPhone-style PWA. The owner can search, speak, approve, inspect and control all companies without opening a terminal.

## Non-negotiable controls

- No API keys in source control.
- Company data must not leak between tenants.
- Publishing, payments, deletion and external messages require policy-based approval.
- Every important action creates an audit event.
- AI proposes; deterministic systems validate; humans approve critical actions.
- Cost ceilings are enforced before expensive model or media operations run.

## Product layers

### Owner Command Center

Cross-company KPIs, risks, approvals, cash, priorities and system health.

### Company Operating Systems

Independent operational modules tailored to each business.

### Agent Center

Agent catalog, active tasks, queues, costs, confidence, failures and approvals.

### Automation Fabric

n8n workflows, scheduled jobs, webhooks and integration adapters.

### Data Platform

Supabase/Postgres, object storage, row-level security, audit logs and backups.

### Client and Agency Layer

Multi-tenant onboarding, plans, subscriptions, support, usage and white-label workspaces.
